The Leftover Food Rescue Delivery App is a full-stack web application designed to reduce food waste by connecting restaurants, bakeries, hostels, and food providers with nearby users who can purchase surplus food at discounted prices.

Instead of throwing away perfectly edible food at the end of the day, businesses can upload available leftover meals, and users can browse, order, and collect food before it expires.

The platform promotes:

Food waste reduction
Affordable meals
Sustainable living
Community support
Features

View food details and pricing
Add items to cart

Add food images, prices, quantity, and availability
Manage listed products

Admin Features

Monitor uploaded food listings

Project Goal

The main goal of this application is to minimize daily food wastage while helping users access affordable meals. Restaurants can recover part of their costs, and users benefit from discounted food options.

How It Works
Restaurants upload leftover food items.
Users browse nearby available meals.
Users place an order online.
Restaurants prepare the order for pickup or delivery.
Food gets consumed instead of wasted.
